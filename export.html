Export & Supply Workflow

Structured, standardized steps from local sourcing to international dispatch.

1. Product Sourcing

Identification and procurement of high-grade raw commodities directly from trusted East African agricultural partners.

2. Supplier Coordination

Aligning delivery timing and batch specifications with producers to maintain procurement consistency.

3. Quality Review

Detailed physical evaluation assessing moisture content, bean/pod size uniformities, and grade compliance.

4. Preparation

Sorting, cleaning, and conditioning bulk shipments to prepare them for long-distance transport.

5. Packaging

Utilizing durable, industry-standard export packaging tailored to protect cargo integrity during transit.

6. Documentation

Compiling official Ugandan export documentation, origin clearance certificates, and shipping manifests.

7. Logistics

Coordinating overland transport from Kampala facilities to regional port hubs and loading terminals.

8. International Shipment

Executing ocean or air freight handovers to deliver commodities to buyer destinations globally.
